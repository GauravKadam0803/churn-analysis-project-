🎯 Project Objective

The objective of this project is to analyze customer data to understand the factors that influence customer churn and identify customers who are at a higher risk of leaving the company. Using Python and data analysis techniques, the project focuses on cleaning and exploring the dataset, performing exploratory data analysis (EDA), identifying patterns and trends, and generating actionable business insights.

This project aims to:

- Analyze customer demographics, services, and account information.
- Identify the key factors contributing to customer churn.
- Perform data cleaning, preprocessing, and exploratory data analysis (EDA).
- Visualize customer behavior and churn trends using charts and graphs.
- Discover relationships between customer attributes and churn.
- Provide data-driven recommendations to improve customer retention and reduce churn.
- Demonstrate practical data analytics skills using Python, Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, and Seaborn.


Insights

- Churn Rate: 28.6% | Retention Rate: 71.4%
- Most of the churn is from basic subscription plan – nothing to worry in terms of major revenue impact
- Most of the churn happened in the month of Sep 2024 and, most affected state is Karnataka
- Average Tenure (Days): 1,451 | ARPU is Rs 18.8
- Total Revenue = 395
- Revenue loss due to churn = 74 | CLTV Lost = 2,047
- % Revenue loss = 18%
- Monthly vs annual churn = 55.6% vs 8.3%

Recommendations

- Improve customer retention by targeting high-risk customers with personalized offers.
- Encourage customers to choose long-term contracts through discounts and loyalty rewards.
- Enhance customer support services to increase customer satisfaction.
- Review pricing strategies for customers with high monthly charges.
- Monitor churn regularly using dashboards and key performance indicators (KPIs).
- Build a machine learning model to predict customer churn in advance.
- Continuously update and analyze customer data for better business decisions.
